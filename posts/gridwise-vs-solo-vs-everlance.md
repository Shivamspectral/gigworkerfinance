## Why earnings tracking apps matter beyond just taxes

Mileage apps like Stride and MileIQ focus purely on tax deductions. A different category of app - earnings trackers like Gridwise, Solo, and Everlance - combines mileage with real-time earnings analytics, helping you decide when and where to drive for maximum profit, not just documenting for the IRS.

This comparison looks at three of the most popular options in this category.

## Gridwise

Gridwise connects directly to your gig platform accounts (Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Instacart, and others) to automatically pull in earnings data alongside mileage tracking.

**Strengths:**
- Automatic earnings import from connected platforms, no manual entry
- Shows earnings per hour and per mile, helping identify your most profitable platforms and times
- Heat maps showing demand patterns in your area
- Free tier covers core tracking and mileage features
- Community features showing aggregated local demand trends

**Limitations:**
- Some advanced analytics features require Gridwise Pro subscription (~$9-15/month)
- Mileage tracking accuracy has been reported as slightly less precise than dedicated mileage-only apps in some user reviews

**Best for:** Multi-app drivers who want to optimize which platform to prioritize and when to drive based on real earnings data.

## Solo

Solo focuses specifically on rideshare and delivery drivers with an emphasis on earnings tracking and future income prediction.

**Strengths:**
- Predicts weekly earnings based on your historical patterns and scheduled hours
- Connects multiple gig platforms for a unified income view
- Simple, clean interface focused on the numbers that matter most
- Offers a debit card product (Solo Card) with cash-back style rewards for gas and driving-related purchases

**Limitations:**
- Mileage tracking is less robust compared to Gridwise or dedicated mileage apps
- Smaller user base means fewer community-driven demand insights

**Best for:** Drivers who want strong income visibility and prediction, particularly useful if you need to estimate income for loan or credit applications.

## Everlance

Everlance started as a mileage-focused app and has expanded into broader expense and income tracking for gig and freelance workers.

**Strengths:**
- Very accurate automatic mileage detection using GPS, widely regarded as one of the best in this category
- Simple swipe classification (business or personal) for each trip
- Clean expense tracking alongside mileage
- Detailed, exportable reports formatted well for tax filing

**Limitations:**
- Free tier caps at 30 automatically tracked trips per month - heavy drivers will hit this limit quickly
- Full-featured earnings analytics are less developed compared to Gridwise

**Best for:** Drivers who prioritize accurate mileage and expense tracking above earnings optimization features, particularly those doing under 30 trips monthly who can stay within the free tier.

## Which one should you choose

**If you drive for multiple platforms and want to know which one is actually most profitable for your specific hours and location:** Gridwise's platform comparison and heat map features are the strongest fit.

**If you need to document consistent income for a loan, apartment application, or just want confidence in your weekly earnings pattern:** Solo's prediction and income visibility tools stand out.

**If your top priority is bulletproof mileage documentation for tax purposes and you drive under 30 trips per month:** Everlance's accuracy and clean reporting make it the best fit within its free tier.

**If you drive heavily across multiple apps and want both strong mileage tracking and earnings insight:** Consider pairing Everlance's paid tier (still affordable) for mileage precision with Gridwise's free tier for earnings analytics - using two apps for their specific strengths.

## Setting up whichever app you choose correctly

1. Connect all your active gig platform accounts if the app supports it
2. Enable automatic trip detection and background location permissions
3. Review and classify any unclassified trips daily rather than letting them accumulate
4. Check your weekly earnings-per-hour and earnings-per-mile metrics to identify your genuinely profitable driving windows
5. Export reports monthly, not just at tax time, to catch any tracking gaps early

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use more than one of these apps at the same time?**
Yes, though running multiple GPS-based trip trackers simultaneously can drain your phone battery faster. Many drivers use one primary app and disable background tracking on others.

**Do these apps replace the need for a separate tax mileage log?**
No - they generate the mileage log for you, but you still need to export and retain that report as part of your tax documentation, the same as any dedicated mileage app.

**Is connecting my gig platform accounts to these apps safe?**
These apps typically use read-only API connections or authorized access similar to how budgeting apps connect to bank accounts. Review each app's privacy policy if you have specific concerns.

**Which app has the most accurate mileage for tax purposes?**
Everlance is generally regarded as having the most precise GPS-based automatic mileage detection among gig-focused tracking apps, based on independent driver reviews and comparisons.

## Bottom line

Gridwise excels at helping you decide where and when to drive for maximum earnings across multiple platforms. Solo shines for income prediction and stability visibility. Everlance leads in pure mileage tracking accuracy. Choose based on whether your bigger need is earnings optimization or airtight tax documentation - or use two apps together to cover both strengths.
