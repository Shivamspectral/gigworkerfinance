## Grubhub drivers are independent contractors, just like DoorDash and Uber Eats

If you deliver for Grubhub, you receive a 1099-NEC rather than a W-2, meaning no taxes are withheld from your pay. You are responsible for tracking income, claiming deductions, and paying self-employment tax and income tax yourself.

## Your Grubhub 1099

Grubhub issues a 1099-NEC if you earned $600 or more during the tax year, typically available by January 31 through the Grubhub for Drivers app or by mail. This form reports gross earnings only - deductions are calculated separately when you file.

## Mileage: your biggest deduction as a Grubhub driver

The 2025 IRS standard mileage rate is 70 cents per mile.

**Deductible Grubhub miles:**
- From the moment you go online and available for orders
- Driving to the restaurant to pick up an order
- Driving from the restaurant to the customer
- Miles driven between deliveries while remaining active in the app

**Not deductible:**
- Driving from home to where you begin your shift
- Personal errands during breaks when offline

**Example:** 14,000 business miles x $0.70 = $9,800 deduction.

## Equipment and supplies

- Insulated delivery bags (required for food quality, fully deductible)
- Phone mount and car charger
- A second phone if used exclusively for delivery apps

## Phone and data plan

Deduct the business-use percentage of your monthly phone bill based on how much you use it for the Grubhub app, navigation, and customer contact versus personal use.

## Vehicle costs if using actual expenses

If you choose the actual expense method instead of standard mileage, you can deduct the business-use percentage of:

- Gas
- Maintenance and repairs
- Insurance
- Registration fees

Remember: pick either standard mileage or actual expenses for the year - not both for the same vehicle.

## Parking and tolls

Parking fees and tolls incurred while making deliveries are deductible separately from mileage, whichever method you use for vehicle costs.

## Comparing Grubhub to other delivery platforms for tax purposes

| Aspect | Grubhub | DoorDash | Uber Eats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Worker classification | 1099 contractor | 1099 contractor | 1099 contractor |
| Tax form issued | 1099-NEC | 1099-NEC | 1099-NEC or 1099-K |
| Mileage deduction eligible | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Withholding | None | None | None |

If you drive for multiple apps, combine all income and expenses onto one Schedule C as a single delivery driving business, unless the work is genuinely unrelated.

## Self-employment tax on Grubhub income

Your net profit is subject to 15.3% self-employment tax (Social Security and Medicare) in addition to regular income tax.

**Sample calculation:**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Gross Grubhub income | $18,000 |
| Mileage deduction (8,000 mi) | $5,600 |
| Supplies and phone | $400 |
| Net profit | $12,000 |
| Self-employment tax (15.3%) | $1,693 |

## Quarterly estimated taxes

If your total expected tax liability across all gig income is $1,000 or more, you should make quarterly estimated payments using Form 1040-ES or IRS Direct Pay online. Missing these results in a small underpayment penalty, calculated as interest on the amount owed.

## Common mistakes Grubhub drivers make

- Assuming a smaller side income does not need to be reported
- Not tracking miles for the pickup leg (restaurant to customer), only counting delivery time
- Forgetting to reconcile Grubhub's 1099 with actual bank deposits
- Skipping quarterly payments because the amounts seem too small to matter

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I owe taxes if Grubhub was just weekend side income?**
Yes, if your net self-employment income (from all gig work combined) is $400 or more for the year, you must file and pay self-employment tax.

**Can I deduct meals I buy for myself during a shift?**
No, personal meals are not deductible, even while working. Only food purchased as part of a customer order is a legitimate expense (rare, but applies if you covered a shortage).

**What if I forgot to track mileage for part of the year?**
Use Grubhub's delivery history to estimate distances for that period going forward, and start using a mileage app immediately to avoid the same issue next year.

**Should I file quarterly if I only drive occasionally?**
If your total expected tax bill for the year is under $1,000, you generally are not required to make quarterly payments, though many drivers choose to set aside savings anyway for simplicity.

## Bottom line

Grubhub drivers face the same self-employment tax responsibilities as other delivery platform workers. Track every mile from pickup to drop-off, save receipts for insulated bags and phone costs, and set aside 25 to 30 percent of your earnings for taxes to avoid a surprise bill in April.
